05 — Questions

Things founders
actually ask.

Straight answers. If yours isn’t here, it’s a good reason to book the call.

What does it cost to work with you?

Retainers start at £3,500 / month and scale with scope. Standalone projects — a brand sprint, a tracking rebuild, a landing-page programme — start at £12,000. Media spend is separate and always transparent; we don’t take a percentage of it.

How quickly will we see results?

Paid media gives you a readable signal in 2–4 weeks and a settled picture by day 60. SEO compounds: expect early movement at 90 days and meaningful pipeline at 3–6 months. We publish the target numbers up front so the timeline is never a surprise.

Do we have to leave our current agency?

Not necessarily. Around a third of our clients run us alongside an incumbent — usually creative with one, media with the other. We also run clean transition projects if you’d rather consolidate. Your call, not ours.

Who actually does the work?

The people you meet. Each strategist leads a maximum of six accounts, and no one learns on your budget. The same lead runs your kickoff and your quarterly review — no handover to a delivery pod you’ve never spoken to.

What if it doesn’t work?

You get a formal performance review at day 90 with agreed targets. If we miss them and the plan was followed, you can walk — no notice penalty, no final invoice beyond work delivered. There’s no 12-month tie-in to hide behind.

Do you work with our industry?

Most often B2B SaaS and services, D2C consumer brands, and multi-location retail or hospitality. We avoid direct conflicts where possible and will tell you on the call if we’re already working with a competitor.
